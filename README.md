# EverJust Company

Operations and AI strategy consulting for small businesses.

We diagnose where operations break down, build the systems to fix it, and deploy an AI layer so the business runs without the owner in every loop.

---

## What We Do

Most small businesses run on memory, text messages, and the owner's calendar. That works until it doesn't. We replace that with connected infrastructure: field management software, written SOPs, business communication systems, and AI agents that handle scheduling, billing, follow-up, and outreach.

The work is specific. We build the actual systems and hand them over — not a strategy deck.

## How We Work

Engagements start with a diagnostic conversation to identify the top operational constraint. From there, we scope a 30-day build: infrastructure first, AI layer second. Every engagement ends with a documented handover and a Month 2 roadmap.

We work with businesses that are profitable, have existing operations, and are ready to stop being the bottleneck.
